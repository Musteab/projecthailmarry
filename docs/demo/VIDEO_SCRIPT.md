## Set up before recording

- Open the deployed app's root URL (no `?backend=server`) for the authored 3D city. Local fallback: `http://localhost:5173/` with `pnpm dev` running. This route is the **scripted in-browser exercise**, not the live backend.
- Choose **Sunway City · urban response**, then **Flash flood**, and **Enter command**. Use the bottom-left pause button while talking. Chapter buttons at the bottom jump the local scene forward.
- Keep **Exercise persona** on **Administrator** (or use the operator for response and recovery coordinator for work orders). Close unrelated tabs, hide notifications, record at 1920×1080 if possible, and keep the cursor still except when demonstrating a click.
- Record the screen in short clips and add the narration afterward. Hold every result for 2–3 seconds. The timecodes below are video time, not the simulation clock.
- On Render Free, open the URL before recording and wait for it to load. A sleeping/restarted server loses exercises. The root scene remains the fallback.

## Claims to keep accurate

- Say “imagery observation in this exercise,” not “live satellite flood detection.” No real image is analyzed in the scripted scene.
- Say “agency-rule action in the exercise,” not “we shut off the city's power.” Utility and emergency-service adapters are not connected.
- Flood and earthquake are the demonstrated journeys. Hurricane/tornado engine support does not yet have the same polished end-to-end demo; extra scenario tickets are deferred.
- Population, damage, crews and grid topology are synthetic. Roads use OpenStreetMap geometry. The models are illustrative exercises, not calibrated forecasts.
- The bundled flood/quake scenes share repair roads. Use the server-backed Portland view when claiming different repair priorities or hospital destinations.
